# Star Fortress Bridge (Commander Payne's home)

**Arena id:** `star-bridge` · **Time:** deep space · **Mood:** big sci-fi showdown

## Lore
The command deck of the Star Fortress *Iron Halo*, hidden behind the glow-in-the-dark stars on the bedroom ceiling. From up here Commander Payne watches the whole toy box through a giant round viewport. If you look closely out the window, you can see the bedroom far below: a tiny lamp, a tiny bed and the block fortress.

## Backdrop (far scenery)
- A huge **round viewport window** filling the middle and top, gold-trimmed frame, looking out at deep blue-black space with chunky stars.
- Through the window: a big **ringed planet** in purple and orange (left of center) and a small glowing moon.
- **Left and right edges:** banks of **control consoles** with chunky glowing buttons (cyan, gold, red), angled screens showing simple glowing shapes (no text), gunmetal walls with gold trim and hex panels.
- Two tall gunmetal pillars with gold bands framing the window, each with a cyan light strip.
- **Floor line:** the deck meets the wall at about 60%.

## Floor
Gunmetal hexagonal deck plates with thin glowing cyan seams between them and a few gold bolts. Seamless.

## Props (6)
1. A console pedestal with a glowing cyan hologram globe on top
2. A stack of two gunmetal supply crates with gold corner caps
3. A tall beacon pylon with a gold dome light
4. A round gunmetal gravity mine (a sphere with gold bands and a cyan light)
5. A little boxy helper droid on treads with a single round cyan eye
6. A gunmetal railing section with gold posts

## Ambient sound
A deep engine hum, soft computer bleeps, a slow sliding-door *whoosh*, and a big brass-and-synth march in the music.
