# Midnight Pumpkin Patch (Jack-Knife's home)

**Arena id:** `pumpkin-patch` · **Time:** midnight on Halloween · **Mood:** spooky-silly harvest party

## Lore
At the edge of town there's a pumpkin patch where every pumpkin lights itself up at midnight on Halloween. This is where Jack-Knife carved himself. The scarecrow is his best friend (it never says anything back, which Jack-Knife likes, because he does all the talking). Rows of grinning jack-o'-lanterns watch every battle and glow brighter when somebody lands a big hit.

## Backdrop (far scenery)
- A dark purple-indigo night sky with a huge **pale green-white full moon**, center-left, partly behind wispy clouds. A few little bat silhouettes near the moon.
- **Left edge:** a crooked old red barn with one warmly lit window and a hayloft door.
- **Right edge:** a bare twisty tree with a swing tire and a crooked **scarecrow** on a post, wearing a straw hat.
- **Middle:** rolling rows of the patch going back into the distance, dotted with **glowing jack-o'-lanterns** (orange glows, a few green ones), a split-rail fence across the back.
- **Floor line:** dark soil meeting the patch rows at about 60%.

## Floor
Dark brown tilled soil with curly green vines, a few fallen orange and red leaves, small pebbles. Seamless.

## Props (6)
1. A pile of three pumpkins, the top one carved and glowing green
2. A hay bale with a pitchfork leaning on it
3. A crooked wooden signpost (arrow-shaped, blank, no letters)
4. A short split-rail fence section with a vine on it
5. A big lone pumpkin carved with a giant grin, glowing orange
6. An old iron lantern on a hook post, glowing green

## Ambient sound
Crickets, a soft wind, an owl hoot, and a bouncy spooky xylophone theme in the music.
