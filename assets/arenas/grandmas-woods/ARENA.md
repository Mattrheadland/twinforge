# Grandma's Woods (Big Bad Wolfgang's home)

**Arena id:** `grandmas-woods` · **Time:** golden late afternoon · **Mood:** storybook adventure

## Lore
The path through the woods to Grandma's cottage, straight out of a picture book. Wolfgang lives in a hollow log just off the trail and waits for anyone carrying a basket of cookies. On the hill behind the trees you can still see the three little pigs' houses: the straw one (flattened), the stick one (flattened) and the brick one (standing tall, with a smug little curl of chimney smoke).

## Backdrop (far scenery)
- A warm golden-orange afternoon sky with puffy cream clouds, low sun upper-right.
- **Left edge:** big chunky storybook oak trees with round leafy crowns, and a **hollow log** at their base (Wolfgang's den).
- **Right edge:** **Grandma's cottage**: white walls, a red roof, round windows with flower boxes, a curl of chimney smoke.
- **Middle (far, on a green hill):** the three little pigs' houses: a flattened straw pile, a collapsed pile of sticks and a neat little **brick house** still standing.
- A winding dirt path leading back between the trees.
- **Floor line:** the path meets the grass at about 60%.

## Floor
A packed dirt forest path with flat stepping stones, tufts of grass at the edges, fallen leaves and a few acorns. Seamless.
