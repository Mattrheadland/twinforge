# Skull Cove (Capt'n Crookhook's home)

**Arena id:** `skull-cove` · **Time:** moonlit night · **Mood:** pirate adventure, treasure-hunt fun

## Lore
A hidden cove at the far end of the bathtub sea, where Capt'n Crookhook anchors the *Rusty Barnacle* and buries his treasure. The cove is guarded by a huge rock arch shaped like a grinning skull, with two lanterns glowing in its eye holes so the ship can find its way home at night. Fight on the beach, and watch out for the tide.

## Backdrop (far scenery)
- A deep navy night sky with a big pale-yellow full moon upper-left and a few chunky stars.
- **Left edge:** the *Rusty Barnacle*, a toy galleon with a dark-brown hull, a teal stripe, patched cream sails, a little black flag with a gold anchor, and lit portholes.
- **Right edge:** the **skull rock arch**, a big friendly cartoon skull carved out of gray-purple rock, with lanterns glowing warm orange in both eye holes and the sea showing through its open mouth.
- **Middle:** calm moonlit water with a gold moon-glint path, low far islands with a palm silhouette or two.
- **Floor line:** wet sand meeting the water at about 60%.

## Floor
Pale golden beach sand with soft ripples, a few small shells and starfish, darker damp sand near the back edge. Seamless and tileable.
