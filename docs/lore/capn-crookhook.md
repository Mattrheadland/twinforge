# Capt'n Crookhook

**Game id:** `crookhook` · **Home arena:** Skull Cove · **Rival:** Billy Bass and Ragin' Rockfish

> A hook-handed pirate captain who has been trying to catch the two best fish in the toy box for as long as anyone can remember.

## Read-aloud lore (for Luca)
Capt'n Crookhook sails the bathtub sea on his ship, the *Rusty Barnacle*. He has a braided silver beard, a big black hat and an iron hook where his right hand used to be. What happened to his hand? He tells a different story every time. Once it was a shark. Once it was a sea monster. Once it was a very, very grumpy clam.

More than gold, he wants to catch the two most famous fish in the toy box: Billy Bass, so he can sing sea shanties on the ship every night, and Ragin' Rockfish, the biggest catch in the bay. He hasn't landed either one yet.

His first mate is Squawks, a loud green parrot who dives at anyone who gets too close to the treasure.

## Personality
Loud, proud, a bit of a show-off. Laughs "HAR HAR!" when he lands a hit and stomps his boot when he misses. Bossy, but he plays fair and always shares the treasure with his crew. Villain-funny, never mean.

## Look (design lock)
- Tall, broad-chested pirate captain action figure, about 4.5 heads tall, standing proud with his chest out.
- **Hat:** big black tricorn hat with a gold trim edge, a long teal plume curling off the back and a small gold anchor badge on the front.
- **Face:** tan skin, a big round nose, bushy silver eyebrows, a black eyepatch over his right eye with the strap across the hat line. His left eye is sharp sea-blue.
- **Beard:** a long silver beard split into **two thick braids** tied off with gold beads. He has no mustache curls, so the braids are the signature shape.
- **Coat:** a deep teal longcoat to the knees with wide gold cuffs, gold edge trim and two rows of brass buttons. A cream shirt underneath and a wide red sash belt with a gold buckle.
- **Hands:** the right arm ends in a big **iron hook** (dark steel, hard white glint). The left hand holds a short curved cutlass with a gold guard.
- **Legs:** dark brown pants and tall black boots with folded tops and gold buckles.
- **Squawks** (FX sidekick, not on the model): a chunky green parrot with a yellow beak and red wing tips.
- **Palette:** teal `#1F8A8A`, gold `#FFC914`, hero red `#D7263D`, silver `#C4CAD8`, hat black `#22202A`, boot black `#2A2430`.

## Keep it original
Pirate captains with hooks are a storybook archetype. Keep him ours: the **teal coat, braided silver beard, eyepatch and gold anchor badge** are his signature, and his look shouldn't drift toward any film or cartoon pirate.
