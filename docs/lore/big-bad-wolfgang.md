# Big Bad Wolfgang

**Game id:** `wolfgang` · **Home arena:** Grandma's Woods · **Rival:** Remy Roo

> The storybook wolf who huffs and puffs, and has been chased out of the backyard by Remy more times than he can count.

## Read-aloud lore (for Luca)
You know the story. Wolfgang huffed and puffed and blew the straw house down. He huffed and puffed and blew the stick house down. Then he huffed and puffed at the brick house all night long, until he got so dizzy he fell asleep on the doorstep.

Now he practices his huffing every single day, with push-ups, howling and blowing out birthday candles from across the room. His favorite snack is Grandma's cookies, and his least favorite thing in the whole world is Remy Roo, who chases him out of the backyard every time he sneaks in to sniff the barbecue.

He thinks he's the scariest thing in the woods. He's mostly just hungry.

## Personality
Big, blustery, dramatic. Brags a lot, sniffs the air before every move, and gets flustered when things don't go his way. Silly-villain, never scary: his grin is goofy, not hungry.

## Look (design lock)
- A big, barrel-chested wolf action figure standing upright on two legs, about 4 heads tall, slightly hunched, with huge feet and hands.
- **Fur:** slate blue-gray (`#6B7A99`) with a darker back stripe, a cream muzzle, chest and inner ears, and a big bushy tail with a cream tip. A little tuft of fur sticks up between his ears.
- **Face:** long snout with a big black nose, tall pointed ears, bright **gold-yellow eyes** with heavy dark brows, and a wide goofy grin showing a neat row of blocky teeth (two slightly bigger front canines).
- **Outfit:** a red-and-black **buffalo-plaid flannel shirt** with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, brown **suspenders**, patched blue **denim overalls cut off at the knee** with one square orange patch on the knee. A **piece of straw** sticks out of the shirt pocket (from the first house).
- **Feet and hands:** big bare paws with chunky cream claws.
- **Palette:** slate `#6B7A99`, cream `#F2E6C8`, hero red `#D7263D`, plaid black `#22202A`, denim `#1B4FD8`, suspender brown `#7A4A22`, eye gold `#FFC914`.

## Battle words and taunts
Intro: "I'LL HUFF… AND I'LL PUFF…" · Win: "AWOOOOO!" · Lose: "Not by the hair of my chinny-chin-chin…"

## Sound notes
A big inhale (rising noise sweep), a wind whoosh with straw rustle, a cartoon howl (pitch-bent sine), a soft *baa* for the sheep disguise, a sniff-sniff before moves.

## Keep it original
The Big Bad Wolf is a public-domain folk tale (*The Three Little Pigs*, *Little Red Riding Hood*). Wolfgang's signature is **the plaid flannel, suspenders, cut-off patched overalls and the straw in the pocket**. Don't dress him in a nightgown or bonnet, and don't base him on any studio's cartoon wolf.
