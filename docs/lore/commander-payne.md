# Commander Payne

**Game id:** `payne` · **Home arena:** Star Fortress Bridge · **Rival:** Young Arthur

> The armored star tyrant who rules the battle station hidden behind the glow-in-the-dark stars on the bedroom ceiling.

## Read-aloud lore (for Luca)
Look up at the glow-in-the-dark stars on the ceiling. Behind the biggest one, too small to see, is the Star Fortress *Iron Halo*, and Commander Payne is in charge of it.

Payne wants every toy in the toy box to join his Star Legion and march in perfect lines. He wears a heavy gold-and-gunmetal suit of armor, flies on shoulder rockets and carries a plasma halberd that hums and crackles.

The only toy who has ever told him "no" is Young Arthur. Payne thinks swords pulled out of stones are old-fashioned. Arthur thinks Payne needs better manners. Every time the Star Fortress drifts over the castle, they battle.

## Personality
Booming, serious, very formal ("COMMENCE BATTLE."). Counts down before his big moves. Hates messes and losing. Big and imposing, but it's all bluster: when he's dizzy he salutes the wrong way. Never scary.

## Look (design lock)
- A hulking armored space-warlord action figure, about 4.5 heads tall: broad shoulders, thick arms, a narrow waist and wide armored boots. A heavy, blocky silhouette.
- **Helmet:** a **round dome helmet** in gunmetal with a **single wraparound visor band glowing gold** across the eyes (expressions show as glowing shapes inside the band). Two short swept-back fins on the sides and a gold ridge running front to back over the top.
- **Armor:** gunmetal gray (`#4A5262`) plates with **gold trim** (`#FFC914`) on every edge. A big chest plate with a raised **gold eight-point star emblem** in the center. A segmented ab plate, a wide gold belt.
- **Shoulders:** large rounded pauldrons, each with a short **rocket thruster** on the back that glows **electric cyan** (`#4EE8FF`) at the nozzle.
- **Arms and hands:** chunky gauntlets with gold knuckle plates.
- **Weapon:** a tall **plasma halberd**: gunmetal shaft with gold grip rings, a crescent axe head made of **crackling cyan energy** and a short spike on top.
- **No cape.** Thrusters and a chunky backpack instead.
- **Palette:** gunmetal `#4A5262` / dark `#2E333F`, gold `#FFC914`, electric cyan `#4EE8FF`, visor gold-glow `#FFD84A`.

## Stats
HP **118** · Attack bonus **+1** · Foe pattern: power → charge → slam → quick
The tank: highest health of the four and a reflect guard. Slow and steady.

## Battle words and taunts
Intro: "COMMENCE BATTLE." · Win: "RESISTANCE IS… NOT ALLOWED." · Lose: "RETREAT! RETREAT!"

## Sound notes
A low hum for the halberd (detuned saw with a slow wobble), a gravity *vwoom* sweep, a shield chime plus electric zap, a countdown beep-beep-beep and a beam blast, heavy servo-step *thunks*. **No breathing sounds** (keeps him clear of any film villain).

## Keep it original
"Dark space knight" is a broad sci-fi archetype. Payne's signature is **gunmetal and gold, the round dome helmet with a single glowing gold visor band, the cyan shoulder rockets and the crescent plasma halberd**. Never black armor with a cape, never a red beam sword, no chest control panel, no breathing sounds.
