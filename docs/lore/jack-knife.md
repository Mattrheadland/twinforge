# Jack-Knife

**Game id:** `jackknife` · **Home arena:** Midnight Pumpkin Patch · **Rival:** Spooky Tree

> A lanky toy skeleton with a jack-o'-lantern for a head, who folds up like a pocketknife and springs right back.

## Read-aloud lore (for Luca)
Every Halloween, the biggest pumpkin in the Midnight Patch gets carved. One year, the biggest pumpkin got tired of waiting and carved **himself**. Then he rummaged through the Halloween decorations bin, found a box of spare skeleton bones and clicked them together into a body. He named himself Jack-Knife, because he can fold up flat like a pocketknife and spring back out, *SNIKT!*

Jack-Knife loves pranks, cartwheels and riddles. His grin glows ghost-green, and he thinks green is the spookiest color there is. Spooky Tree says orange is spookier. They have argued about it every Halloween since, and the only way to settle it is a battle.

## Personality
Bouncy, giggly, a trickster. Talks in riddles, cartwheels when he wins and pretends to fall apart when he's hit (the bones always click back). Spooky-silly, never scary.

## Look (design lock)
- Lanky, long-limbed toy skeleton action figure, about 4 heads tall (counting the pumpkin), knees and elbows a little bent, springy stance.
- **Head:** a round orange carved pumpkin with deep vertical ridges, a curly green stem with one leaf on top. Carved triangle eyes and a wide jagged zig-zag grin, **glowing ghost-green** from inside, with a soft green glow on the pumpkin's edges.
- **Bones:** chunky, rounded ivory toy bones (`#EDE6D2`), knobby joints, a stacked spine visible between the vest flaps, a little ribcage peeking out.
- **Outfit:** a patched **burlap vest** (sandy tan) with two stitched patches, one purple and one green. A twisted **green vine belt** with three little leaves. A short, tattered **purple cape** with ragged points, fastened with a gold pumpkin-shaped clasp.
- **Hands:** long bony fingers. He carries nothing, since his tricks are his weapons.
- **Feet:** chunky bone feet with one mismatched sock: a purple-and-green striped sock on the left foot only.
- **Palette:** pumpkin orange `#F46036` / `#E8762B`, ghost green `#5CF2A0`, ivory `#EDE6D2`, burlap `#C9A15A`, royal purple `#6A3FA0`, vine green `#2E9E44`.

## Stats
HP **98** · Attack bonus **+3** · Foe pattern: power → quick → charge → slam
Glass cannon: low health, hits hard, and his dodge is his lifeline.

## Battle words and taunts
Intro: "TRICK… OR TREAT?" · Win: "HEE HEE HEE!" · Lose: "I'm all in pieces!"

## Sound notes
Bone clatter (wooden xylophone rattle), a *snikt* fold, a hollow pumpkin *thonk*, a spooky slide-whistle for the barrage, a giggle stinger.

## Keep it original
Pumpkin heads and skeletons are classic Halloween. Jack-Knife's signature is **the carved pumpkin head with the green glow, the burlap vest with patches, the vine belt and the tattered purple cape**. Keep him in that outfit, not a suit, and keep his head a pumpkin, never a skull.
