# 003: First Play goes straight into a guided fight

Context: the first fight took 6-8 taps through text menus, and the first fight could be lost.
Decision: a lone brand-new player's first Play starts a guided battle (Battle Rex vs Spooky Tree) that can't be lost and speaks each step. One tap stays, because phones need a tap before sound and speech.
Consequences: this skips "who's playing?" (Founder rule, 2026-10-16) for that one fight only; households with several players are unchanged. The guided fight counts as a normal win.
