# 002: L1-L6 ladder with per-strand ratings

Context: content ended near grade 1 and difficulty moved as one number per subject, so a child strong at adding but weak at take-away was held back on both.
Decision: levels 1-8 shown as L1-L6, with an Older learner (8-10) pathway; new types use 4 choices. Each question kind keeps an Elo-style rating that picks which kind to ask and quietly eases a struggling one by a level. The subject level still steps on the old rules (3 first-try in a row up, 2 full-help in 4 down).
Consequences: Growing can now reach L4. Saves gain mem.elo (old saves load with none). Reviews of a missed question come back at the subject level, not eased. Item difficulties are fixed by level for now; learning them needs ~100+ players.
